General Hosting & Server FAQs

How fast is my cloud hosting account provisioned?

All cloud VPS and shared hosting orders are provisioned instantly upon payment verification. You will receive your access credentials and control panel login details within minutes.

Do you provide free SSL certificates with all packages?

Yes! Every hosting package includes complimentary Let's Encrypt SSL certificates that automatically renew every 90 days with zero downtime.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my hosting tier anytime?

Absolutely. You can seamlessly scale CPU cores, RAM, and SSD storage directly from your client control panel with zero reboot time in most configurations.

What backup and disaster recovery policies are in place?

We execute automated daily snapshots stored redundantly off-site. You can restore your entire filesystem or specific MySQL databases with a single click at any time.

What kind of uptime guarantee do you offer?

We back all of our enterprise and shared cloud solutions with an industry-leading 99.99% Service Level Agreement (SLA).

Domains & Billing Inquiries

Can I transfer my existing domain to Cloud Store?

Yes, domain transfers are quick and straightforward. Simply unlock your domain at your current registrar, get the EPP transfer code, and our system will handle the rest.

Which payment methods do you support?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and wire transfers for annual corporate contracts.
